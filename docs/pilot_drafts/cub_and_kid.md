# የነብር ግልገልና የፍየል ግልገል

English review title: **The Cub and the Kid**
Origin basis: Amhara story narrated by Magabi Enyew Gessesse
Draft audience: ages 5-6
Draft length: 11 pages

Review record: owner language review completed 2026-07-02 with no changes
requested. Provisionally accepted pending real-family read-aloud and trust/
safety review. Text is not locked.

## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ አረንጓዴ ሸለቆ ውስጥ የነብር ግልገል ከእናቷ ጋር ትኖር ነበር። በአቅራቢያውም የፍየል ግልገል ከመንጋዋ ጋር ትኖር ነበር።

**English**

In a green valley, a leopard cub lived with her mother. Nearby, a young goat lived with her herd.

**Illustration beat:** Valley showing cub on one side and kid near herd on the other.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

አንድ ጠዋት ሁለቱም ቢራቢሮ እየተከተሉ ወንዙ አጠገብ ተገናኙ። ከዚህ በፊት አይተዋወቁም ነበር።

**English**

One morning they followed the same butterfly and met beside the river. They had never seen each other before.

**Illustration beat:** Cub and kid meeting with butterfly between them.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

“ከእኔ ጋር መጫወት ትፈልጊያለሽ?” ብላ የነብሯ ግልገል ጠየቀች። የፍየሏ ግልገልም “እሺ!” ብላ በደስታ መለሰች።

**English**

“Would you like to play with me?” asked the cub. “Yes!” answered the kid happily.

**Illustration beat:** Both animals smiling cautiously, ready to play.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

በሣሩ ላይ ሮጡ፣ ከድንጋይ ወደ ድንጋይ ዘለሉ፣ የወንዙንም ድምፅ አዳመጡ። ፀሐይ ስትጠልቅ “ነገ እንገናኝ” ተባብለው ወደ ቤታቸው ሄዱ።

**English**

They raced through the grass, hopped from stone to stone, and listened to the river. At sunset they promised to meet again and went home.

**Illustration beat:** Joyful play beside river in late-afternoon light.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

የነብሯ ግልገል ስለ አዲሷ ጓደኛዋ ለእናቷ ነገረቻት። እናቷ ግን “ነብሮችና ፍየሎች ብዙ ጊዜ ጓደኛሞች አይሆኑም” አለቻት።

**English**

The cub told her mother about her new friend. Her mother looked serious. “Leopards and goats do not usually become friends,” she said.

**Illustration beat:** Cub speaking with serious mother leopard at den.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

የፍየሏ ግልገልም የነብሯን ነጠብጣብ እና ጨዋታቸውን ለእናቷ ነገረቻት። “ልጄ፣ ነብር አዳኝ ነው። ነገ ብቻሽን ወደዚያ አትሂጂ” አለቻት እናቷ።

**English**

The kid told her mother about the cub's spots and their games. “My child, a leopard is a hunter. Do not go there alone tomorrow,” her mother warned.

**Illustration beat:** Mother goat giving calm safety advice beside the herd.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

በማግስቱ የነብሯ ግልገል ወንዙ አጠገብ ጠበቀች። የፍየሏ ግልገል ግን ከመንጋዋ አጠገብ ቆማ “ዛሬ ወደዚያ አልመጣም” አለቻት።

**English**

The next day the cub waited by the river. The kid stayed close to her herd. “I will not come over today,” she called.

**Illustration beat:** Safe distance between riverbank cub and kid beside herd.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

“ትናንት አብረን ደስ ብሎን ነበር። ምን ተለወጠ?” ብላ ጠየቀች የነብሯ ግልገል። የፍየሏ ግልገልም “እናቶቻችን ስለ ተፈጥሯችን ነግረውናል። ደህንነቴን መጠበቅ አለብኝ” አለች።

**English**

“We had such fun yesterday. What changed?” asked the cub. “Our mothers told us about our nature,” said the kid. “I need to keep myself safe.”

**Illustration beat:** Kid speaking clearly while cub listens from across water.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

የነብሯ ግልገል ትንሽ አዘነች፣ ነገር ግን ቀርባ አላስፈራራቻትም። “እሺ፣ የምትፈልጊውን ርቀት እጠብቃለሁ” አለቻት።

**English**

The cub felt sad, but she did not come closer or frighten the kid. “All right,” she said. “I will respect the distance you need.”

**Illustration beat:** Cub sitting down peacefully, leaving clear space.

## Page 10

**Amharic**

ሁለቱም በወንዙ ተቃራኒ ዳርቻ ሆነው ቢራቢሮዋን እንደገና አዩ። ሳይቀራረቡ እየሮጡ በእያንዳንዱ ዳርቻ ተከተሏት።

**English**

Then the butterfly returned. From opposite sides of the river, they ran along their own banks and followed it without coming too close.

**Illustration beat:** Parallel play along opposite banks, butterfly overhead.

## Page 11

**Amharic**

ጓደኝነታቸው እንደ ትናንቱ አልነበረም፤ ግን ሁለቱም አንድ ነገር ተማሩ። ደግነት የሌላውን ደህንነትና ድንበር ማክበርም ነው።

**English**

Their friendship could not be the same as before, but they both learned something: kindness also means respecting another person's safety and boundaries.

**Illustration beat:** Both animals resting safely with families visible nearby.
