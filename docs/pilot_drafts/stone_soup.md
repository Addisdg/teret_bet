## Page 1

**Amharic**

አንድ መንገደኛ ረጅም መንገድ ተጉዞ በምሽት ወደ ትንሽ መንደር ደረሰ። በጣም ደክሞት እና ተርቦም ነበር።

**English**

After a long journey, a traveller reached a small village in the evening. He was very tired and hungry.

**Illustration beat:** Tired traveller approaching a quiet village at sunset.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

በመንደሩ እየዞረ የብዙ ሰዎችን በር አንኳኳ። “የምታካፍሉኝ ትንሽ ምግብ አላችሁ?” ብሎ ጠየቀ። ሰዎቹ ግን “ያለን ለቤተሰባችን ብቻ የሚበቃ ነው” አሉት።

**English**

He walked around the village and knocked on many doors. “Do you have a little food to share with me?” he asked. “What we have is only enough for our families,” the villagers replied.

**Illustration beat:** Several villagers answering cautiously but not harshly.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

ተጓዡም አላዘነም። በመንደሩ መካከል ያለውን የውሃ ጉድጓድ አይቶ “እንግዲያው የድንጋይ ሾርባ እሠራለሁ” አለ።

**English**

The traveller did not become discouraged. He noticed the village well. “Then I shall make stone soup,” he said.

**Illustration beat:** Traveller smiling thoughtfully beside the well.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

ንጹሕ ድንጋይ አጥቦ በትልቅ ማሰሮ ውስጥ አስቀመጠ። ውሃ ጨመረበትና ትንሽ እሳት አቀጣጠለ።

**English**

He washed a clean stone and placed it in a large pot. He added water and lit a small cooking fire.

**Illustration beat:** Clean stone dropping into pot over safe cooking fire.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

የአካባቢው ልጆች በጉጉት ወደ ተጓዡ ቀረቡ። “ድንጋይ እንዴት ሾርባ ይሆናል?” አሉ። ተጓዡም “ከእናንተ ትንሽ እርዳታ ጋር ጣፋጭ ይሆናል” ብሎ ፈገግ አለ።

**English**

The local children approached the traveller curiously. “How can a stone become soup?” they asked. “With a little help from you, it can become delicious,” the traveller said with a smile.

**Illustration beat:** Curious children peering safely into pot.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

አንዲት ልጅ “እኔ ሁለት ካሮት ማምጣት እችላለሁ” አለች። እናቷን ጠይቃ ካሮቶቹን አመጣች፣ ሁሉም አብረው ወደ ማሰሮው ጨመሯቸው።

**English**

“I can bring two carrots,” said one child. She asked her mother, brought the carrots, and everyone added them to the pot together.

**Illustration beat:** Child and parent contributing carrots with consent.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

አንድ ጎረቤት ትንሽ ድንች፣ ሌላው ደግሞ በቆሎ አመጣ። አንዲት አያትም “ከጓሮዬ ትንሽ ቅጠል አለኝ” ብለው ጨመሩ።

**English**

One neighbour brought a potato, and another brought some maize. A grandmother added, “I have a few herbs from my garden.”

**Illustration beat:** Different generations bringing small ingredients.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ማሰሮው “ፍልቅልቅ፣ ፍልቅልቅ” እያለ መቀቀል ጀመረ። ጣፋጭ ሽታው በመንደሩ ሲሰራጭ ተዘግተው የነበሩ በሮች ተከፈቱ።

**English**

Bubble, bubble went the pot. As its delicious smell drifted through the village, doors that had been closed began to open.

**Illustration beat:** Steam curling through village as doors open.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

አንዳንዶቹ ውኃ ቀዱ፣ አንዳንዶቹ ሳህን አዘጋጁ፣ ሌሎቹም ጠረጴዛ ዘረጉ። ትንሽ ትንሽ እየሠሩ ትልቅ እራት አዘጋጁ።

**English**

Some fetched water, some set out bowls, and others prepared a long table. Many small acts were turning into one big supper.

**Illustration beat:** Villagers sharing different preparation jobs.

## Page 10

**Amharic**

ተጓዡ ሾርባውን ቀምሶ “አንድ ነገር ቀርቶታል” አለ። ሁሉም በጉጉት ተመለከቱት። “አብረን መቀመጥ!” ብሎ ሳቀ።

**English**

The traveller tasted the soup. “One thing is still missing,” he said. Everyone leaned closer. “We must sit down together!” he laughed.

**Illustration beat:** Traveller's playful reveal; villagers laughing with relief.

## Page 11

**Amharic**

ሁሉም በጠረጴዛው ዙሪያ ተቀምጠው ሞቅ ያለውን ሾርባ ተካፈሉ። “ይህን ሁሉ ያዘጋጀው ድንጋዩ ነው?” ብሎ አንድ ልጅ ጠየቀ።

**English**

Everyone sat around the table and shared the warm soup. “Did the stone make all of this?” one child asked.

**Illustration beat:** Warm communal table; child pointing curiously toward pot.

## Page 12

**Amharic**

“ድንጋዩ የጀመረው ጨዋታውን ብቻ ነው፤ ሾርባውን ያዘጋጀው የእያንዳንዳችሁ ትንሽ ስጦታ ነው” አለ ተጓዡ። ከዚያ ቀን በኋላ የመንደሩ ነዋሪዎች ትንሽ ነገር ሲካፈሉት ለሁሉም እንደሚበቃ ተገነዘቡ።

**English**

“The stone only started the game,” said the traveller. “The soup came from each small gift you shared.” From then on, the villagers understood that when they shared even a little, there could be enough for everyone.

**Illustration beat:** Stone resting beside empty bowls as community smiles together.
