# ቀድማ የተዘጋጀችው አይጠ መጎጥ

English review title: **The Rat Who Prepared Early**
Origin basis: Wolayita story collected from Yisihak Aldade
Draft audience: ages 3-4
Draft length: 9 pages

Review record: owner language review completed 2026-07-02 with wording and
character-translation changes. `አይጠ መጎጥ` is represented as a small rat in
English and in every illustration brief. Provisionally accepted pending real-
family read-aloud and age-fit review. Text is not locked.

## Page 1

**Amharic**

በሞቃታማ ሜዳ ውስጥ አንዲት ትንሽ አይጠ መጎጥ ትኖር ነበር። በየጠዋቱ ከቤቷ ወጥታ ፀሐይ ትሞቅ ነበር።

**English**

A little rat lived in a warm, grassy place. Each morning she stepped outside and warmed herself in the sun.

**Illustration beat:** A small rat warming herself in the morning sun beside her grass home.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

አንድ ቀን ጎረቤቶቿ የዝናብ ወቅት ሳይደርስ ቤታቸውን ሲጠግኑ አየቻቸው። “ክረምቱ ገና ብዙ ይቀረዋል፤ ለምን ገና ከአሁኑ መስራት ትጀምራላችሁ?” ብላ ጠየቀቻቸው።

**English**

One day she saw her neighbours repairing their homes long before the rainy season. “The rains are still a long way off. Why are you starting your work already?” she asked.

**Illustration beat:** Neighbouring animals mending roofs while the small rat watches.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

“ቀድመን ከተዘጋጀን ዝናቡ ሲመጣ ደረቅና ሞቅ ብለን እንቆያለን” አሏት። አይጠ መጎጧም በጥሞና አዳመጠቻቸው።

**English**

“If we prepare early, we will stay warm and dry when the rain comes,” they told her. The rat listened carefully.

**Illustration beat:** Friendly neighbours explaining while pointing to a sturdy roof.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

በማግስቱ ቀትር ፀሐይ በኃይል ስትበራ፣ አይጠ መጎጧ በጥላ ውስጥ ቆማ “ብርር... ብርር...” እያለች መንቀጥቀጥ ጀመረች።

**English**

At noon the next day, while the sun shone brightly, the rat stood in the shade and began to shiver. “Brrr... brrr...”

**Illustration beat:** The small rat playfully shivering in the shade on a hot day.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

አንድ እረኛ አይቶ “ፀሐይ እንዲህ ስትሞቅ ለምን ትንቀጠቀጫለሽ?” ብሎ ጠየቃት። አይጠ መጎጧም ፈገግ አለች።

**English**

A shepherd saw her. “Why are you shivering when the sun is so warm?” he asked. The rat smiled.

**Illustration beat:** Curious shepherd speaking gently to the smiling rat.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

“ቀዝቃዛው ወቅት ሲመጣ እንዳልደነግጥ ከአሁኑ ጀምሬ እየተለማመድኩ ነው” አለችው። እረኛው ትንሽ አሰበና ሳቀ።

**English**

“I am practising now, so the cold season will not surprise me,” she said. The shepherd thought for a moment, then laughed.

**Illustration beat:** The rat proudly explaining her funny plan.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

“መዘጋጀት ጥሩ ነው፤ ነገር ግን መንቀጥቀጥ ብቻ አያሞቅሽም፤ በይ ነይ ሞቅ ያለ ጎጆ አብረን እንሥራ።” አላት።

**English**

“Preparing early is wise, but shivering alone will not keep you warm. Come, let us build a cosy home together,” he said.

**Illustration beat:** Shepherd offering reeds and dry grass for a cosy home.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

አይጠ መጎጧ ደረቅ ሣር ሰበሰበች፣ እረኛውም ትንሽ ጣሪያዋን አጠናከረላት። ጎረቤቶቿም መጥተው በደስታ አገዟት።

**English**

The rat gathered dry grass, and the shepherd strengthened her little roof. Her neighbours came gladly to help.

**Illustration beat:** Community building a warm grass-lined home together.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

ቀዝቃዛው ወቅት ሲደርስ አይጠ መጎጧ በሞቀ ጎጆዋ ውስጥ ተቀመጠች። “ቀድሞ መዘጋጀት ጥሩ ነው፤ ትክክለኛውን ነገር ማዘጋጀት ደግሞ የበለጠ ጥሩ ነው!” ብላ ሳቀች።

**English**

When the cold season arrived, the rat sat snug inside her warm home. “Preparing early is good,” she laughed, “and preparing the right thing is even better!”

**Illustration beat:** The small rat warm inside while soft rain falls outside.

## Review questions

- `አይጠ መጎጥ` is approved. Translate it as “rat” and depict a small rodent in
  every illustration, never a deer-like animal or squirrel.
- Does changing the original joke into practical preparation preserve enough of
  the Wolayita story's identity?
- Does page 6 sound natural and playful when read aloud?
