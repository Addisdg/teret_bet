# የአይጦቹ ንጉሥ ልጅ

## Page 1

**Amharic**

ከትልቅ ተራራ ሥር ብዙ አይጦች በአንድነት ይኖሩ ነበር። ንጉሣቸውም ልጁን እጅግ ይወደው ነበር።

**English**

Beneath a great mountain lived a large community of rats. Their king loved his son very much.

**Illustration beat:** Rat community beneath a mountain; king beside young prince.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

አንድ ቀን ንጉሡ “ልጄ ሲያድግ ጥሩ ቤተሰብ እንዲመሠርት እፈልጋለሁ። በዓለም ላይ ከሁሉ ታላቅ የሆነውን ቤተሰብ እንፈልግ” አለ።

**English**

One day the king said, “When my son grows up, I want him to build a good family. Let us seek the greatest family in the world.”

**Illustration beat:** King speaking to a circle of rat elders.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

ሽማግሌዎቹ “ከፈጣሪ የሚበልጥ ማን አለ?” ብለው ወደ ሰማይ ተመለከቱ። ንጉሡም “እንግዲያው ፈጣሪን እንጠይቅ” አለ።

**English**

“Who could be greater than the Creator?” the elders wondered, looking toward the sky. “Then we shall ask the Creator,” said the king.

**Illustration beat:** Elders gazing upward from the foot of the mountain.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

ፈጣሪ በትሕትና ሰማቸውና “ደመና ሲመጣ ሰማዩን ይሸፍናል። ምናልባት ደመናውን ትጠይቁ ይሆናል” አላቸው።

**English**

The Creator listened kindly. “When Cloud arrives, it covers the sky. Perhaps you should ask Cloud,” came the answer.

**Illustration beat:** A gentle cloud spreading across the sky.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

አይጦቹ ወደ ደመናው ሄዱ። “አንተ ከሰማይ ይልቅ ታላቅ ነህን?” ብለው ጠየቁት።

**English**

The rats hurried to Cloud. “Are you greater than the sky?” they asked.

**Illustration beat:** Tiny rat elders beneath a soft, low cloud.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ደመናውም “እኔ ሰማዩን እሸፍናለሁ፤ ነፋስ ግን ሲነፍስ ይበትነኛል” አለ። “ከእኔ ይልቅ ነፋስን ጠይቁ።”

**English**

“I can cover the sky,” said Cloud, “but Wind scatters me whenever it blows. Ask Wind, who is stronger than I am.”

**Illustration beat:** Wind gently parting the cloud as rats hold their hats.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

አይጦቹ ነፋስን ተከትለው “አንተ ከሁሉ ታላቅ ነህን?” አሉት። ነፋሱ ዙሪያቸውን እየዞረ ሳቀ።

**English**

The rats followed the breeze. “Are you the greatest of all?” they asked. Wind swirled around them with a laugh.

**Illustration beat:** Playful wind curling around travelling rat elders.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

“ደመናን ማባረር እችላለሁ፤ ተራራን ግን ከቦታው ማንቀሳቀስ አልችልም” አለ ነፋሱ። “ከእኔ ይልቅ ተራራን ጠይቁ” አላቸው።

**English**

“I can chase Cloud away,” said Wind, “but I cannot move Mountain from its place. Ask Mountain, who is greater than I am.”

**Illustration beat:** Wind bending trees while the mountain stands calmly.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

ሽማግሌዎቹ ወደ ተራራው ቀርበው “አንተ ከነፋስ ይልቅ ታላቅ ነህን?” አሉ። ተራራውም በጥልቅ ድምፅ መለሰ።

**English**

The elders stood before Mountain. “Are you greater than Wind?” they called. Mountain answered in a deep voice.

**Illustration beat:** Rat elders at the immense but friendly mountain face.

## Page 10

**Amharic**

“ነፋስ አያንቀሳቅሰኝም፤ ነገር ግን ትንሹ የዱር አይጥ በውስጤ መንገድ ይቆፍራል” አለ ተራራው። “እርሱ የማልችለውን ይችላል።”

**English**

“Wind cannot move me,” said Mountain, “yet a little bush rat digs pathways through me. He can do something I cannot.”

**Illustration beat:** Cutaway view of a small bush rat making a tunnel.

## Page 11

**Amharic**

ሽማግሌዎቹ ተደነቁ። በጉዟቸው ሁሉ ከትልቅ ወደ ትልቅ ሲፈልጉ፣ መልሱ ከቤታቸው አጠገብ ነበር።

**English**

The elders were amazed. They had travelled far in search of greatness, only to find the answer close to home.

**Illustration beat:** Elders exchanging surprised smiles near the tunnel entrance.

## Page 12

**Amharic**

ወደ ንጉሡ ተመልሰው ያዩትን ሁሉ ነገሩት። “እያንዳንዱ የሚችለው የተለየ ነገር አለ” አሉት።

**English**

They returned and told the king everything. “Each one has a different kind of strength,” they said.

**Illustration beat:** Elders recounting the journey to king and prince.

## Page 13

**Amharic**

ንጉሡ ከዱር አይጦቹ ቤተሰብ ጋር ተዋወቀ። ልጆቻቸውም አብረው ሲጫወቱና ሲማማሩ ደስ አላቸው።

**English**

The king met the bush-rat family. Their children enjoyed playing, learning, and spending time together.

**Illustration beat:** Two rat families sharing food while the young rats play.

## Page 14

**Amharic**

ዓመታት ካለፉ በኋላ ልጆቹ አድገው ቤተሰብ መሠረቱ። ንጉሡም “ታላቅነት ሌሎችን በመብለጥ ሳይሆን ያለንን መልካም ነገር በማወቅ ነው” ብሎ ተማረ።

**English**

Years later, the young rats grew up and made a family of their own. The king learned that greatness is not being above everyone else; it is recognising the good each one carries.

**Illustration beat:** Grown rat couple with both families celebrating warmly.

## Review questions

- The family/marriage frame is provisionally accepted and should be checked
  again during real-family review.
- `ደመና` is the approved Amharic term for the cloud/fog character.
- Does the Creator -> Cloud -> Wind -> Mountain -> Bush Rat sequence need another
  transition page anywhere?
- The draft changes boastful language into a gentler search for greatness. Does
  that preserve the traditional story adequately?
