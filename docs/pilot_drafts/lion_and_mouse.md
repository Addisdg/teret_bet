## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ ሞቃት ቀን አንድ ትልቅ አንበሳ በግራር ዛፍ ጥላ ሥር ተኝቶ ነበር። ነፋሱ ጎፈሩን በቀስታ ያወዛውዘው ነበር።

**English**

One warm day, a great lion slept beneath an acacia tree. The breeze gently stirred his mane.

**Illustration beat:** Sleeping lion beneath acacia in warm grassland.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

በአቅራቢያው አንድ ትንሽ አይጥ ጥራ ጥሬ እየሰበሰበ ነበር። አንድ ጥሬ ተንከባሎ ወደ አንበሳው እግር ሄደ፣ አይጡም ተከተለው።

**English**

Nearby, a little mouse was gathering grain. One grain rolled beside the lion's paw, and the mouse hurried after it.

**Illustration beat:** A grain rolling toward the lion while the mouse follows.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

በድንገት አይጡ ሳያውቅ የአንበሳውን ጅራት ረገጠ። አንበሳውም ደንግጦ ነቃና በትልቅ መዳፉ መንገዱን ዘጋበት።

**English**

Suddenly, without realising it, the mouse stepped on the lion's tail. The startled lion woke and blocked the mouse's path with one enormous paw.

**Illustration beat:** Surprised lion and startled mouse; no threatening claws.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

“እባክህ አያ አንበሳ ሆይ ይቅር በለኝ፤ ላስደነግጥህ አልፈለግሁም” አለ አይጡ። “ምናልባትም አንድ ቀን እኔም ልረዳህ እችላለሁ።”

**English**

“Please forgive me, Father Lion. I did not mean to startle you,” said the mouse. “Perhaps one day I can help you too.”

**Illustration beat:** Mouse speaking bravely while lion listens.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

አንበሳው ትንሹን አይጥ ተመለከተና ፈገግ አለ። “ትንሽ ብትሆንም ቃልህ ትልቅ ነው። ሂድ፣ በሰላም” ብሎ መንገዱን ለቀቀለት።

**English**

The lion studied the tiny mouse and smiled. “You may be small, but that is a large promise. Go in peace.” He lifted his paw.

**Illustration beat:** Lion lifting paw and mouse leaving gratefully.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ከጥቂት ቀናት በኋላ አንበሳው በጫካው ዳር ሲሄድ ድንገት በወደቁ ገመዶችና ቅርንጫፎች መካከል ተጠላልፎ ወደቀ። ራሱን ለመፍታት ሞከረ፣ ነገር ግን ገመዱ ይበልጥ እያጣበቀው መጣ።

**English**

A few days later, while walking along the forest's edge, the lion suddenly tripped and fell among some ropes and branches. He tried to free himself, but the ropes tightened around him.

**Illustration beat:** Lion gently tangled, visibly uncomfortable but safe.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

“የሚሰማኝ አለ?” ብሎ አንበሳው መጣራት ጀመረ። ድምፁ በሜዳው ላይ አስተጋባ፣ ትንሹ አይጥም ጆሮ ላይ ደረሰ።

**English**

“Can anyone hear me?” the lion began to call. His voice echoed across the grassland and reached the little mouse's ears.

**Illustration beat:** Sound travelling across grass to attentive mouse.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

አይጡ በፍጥነት ሮጦ ደረሰ። “አትፍራ፣ ጓደኛዬ። ትንንሽ ጥርሶቼ ለዚህ ጊዜ በጣም ይጠቅማሉ” ብሎ ገመዱን ማኘክ ጀመረ።

**English**

The mouse raced to him. “Do not worry, my friend. My tiny teeth will be very useful now.” He began to nibble through the rope.

**Illustration beat:** Mouse working on rope while lion waits patiently.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

ቀስ በቀስ አንዱ ገመድ ተበጠሰ፣ ከዚያም ሌላው። አንበሳው ቅርንጫፎቹን በጥንቃቄ ገፍቶ ነፃ ወጣ።

**English**

Nibble by nibble, one rope broke, then another. The lion carefully pushed the branches aside and stepped free.

**Illustration beat:** Final rope parting; both characters visibly relieved.

## Page 10

**Amharic**

“ትንሽ ጓደኛዬ፣ ቃልህን ጠብቀሃል” አለ አንበሳው። ከዚያ ቀን ጀምሮ ሁለቱም ደግነት በመጠን እንደማትለካ እያስታወሱ ጓደኛሞች ሆነው ኖሩ።

**English**

“My little friend, you kept your promise,” said the lion. From that day on, they remained friends and remembered that kindness is never measured by size.

**Illustration beat:** Lion and mouse walking together at sunset.
