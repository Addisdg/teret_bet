# ወርቃማ እንቁላል የምትጥለው ዝይ

Review revision: `BATCH2-AESOP-R1-DRAFT-2026-07-08-GOOSE-GOLDEN-EGGS`
Review status: draft text only; not owner-reviewed; not text locked

English review title: **The Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs**
Origin basis: Aesopic public-domain tradition
Source anchor: Project Gutenberg, V. S. Vernon Jones, *Aesop's Fables*, 1912
Draft audience: ages 4-6 provisional
Draft length: 9 pages

Safety note: the traditional source ending kills the goose. This Teret Bet
draft replaces that with the goose leaving the noisy/rushed home for a quiet
meadow. The consequence remains clear without graphic harm.

## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ ትንሽ እርሻ ላይ አንዲት ለስላሳ ዝይ ትኖር ነበር። ቤተሰቡ በየጠዋቱ ውሃና እህል ይሰጣት ነበር።

**English**

On a small farm lived a gentle goose. Each morning, the family gave her water and grain.

**Illustration beat:** Gentle goose in a warm farmyard.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

አንድ ጠዋት ቤተሰቡ በጎጆዋ ውስጥ የሚያበራ ወርቃማ እንቁላል አገኙ። “እንዴት ያማረ ስጦታ ነው!” አሉ።

**English**

One morning, the family found a shining golden egg in her nest. “What a beautiful gift!” they said.

**Illustration beat:** Family discovering one glowing golden egg.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

በሚቀጥለው ቀንም ሌላ ወርቃማ እንቁላል አገኙ። ቤተሰቡ ዝይዋን በደግነት አመሰገኑ።

**English**

The next day, there was another golden egg. The family thanked the goose kindly.

**Illustration beat:** Goose being cared for and thanked.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

ቀኖች እየተከተሉ ሲሄዱ ባለቤቱ ብዙ ነገር መመኘት ጀመረ። “በየቀኑ አንድ ብቻ መጠበቅ ለምን ያስፈልጋል?” ብሎ አሰበ።

**English**

As days passed, the farmer began to want more. “Why should I wait for only one egg each day?” he wondered.

**Illustration beat:** Farmer thinking impatiently while goose rests.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

ቤተሰቡ “ስጦታ በትዕግሥት ይቀበላል። ዝይዋንም በደግነት እንንከባከብ” አሉት።

**English**

His family said, “A gift is received with patience. Let us care for the goose kindly.”

**Illustration beat:** Family gently advising the farmer.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ባለቤቱ ግን ትዕግሥት አጣ። ጎጆዋን በችኮላ ከፈተ፣ ወደ ውስጥም በጩኸት ተመለከተ።

**English**

But the farmer lost patience. He opened her little house in a rush and looked inside noisily.

**Illustration beat:** Farmer rushing; goose startled but safe.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

ዝይዋ ፈራች። ክንፏን አንቀሳቀሰችና ከጎጆዋ ወጥታ ወደ ጸጥ ያለ ሜዳ ሄደች።

**English**

The goose was frightened. She fluttered her wings, left the nest, and went to a quiet meadow.

**Illustration beat:** Goose moving safely toward a peaceful meadow.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ከዚያ ቀን በኋላ በጎጆዋ ውስጥ ወርቃማ እንቁላል አልተገኘም። ባለቤቱ ዝም ብሎ ተቀመጠ።

**English**

After that day, no golden egg appeared in the nest. The farmer sat quietly.

**Illustration beat:** Empty nest; farmer regretful but calm.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

“ደግነትና ትዕግሥት ያለንን ስጦታ ይጠብቃሉ” ብሎ ተማረ። ከዚያ በኋላ ያለውን በምስጋና መቀበል ጀመረ።

**English**

“Kindness and patience protect the gifts we have,” he learned. After that, he began to receive what he had with gratitude.

**Illustration beat:** Farmer caring for the farm humbly at sunset.
