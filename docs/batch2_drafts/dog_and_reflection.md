# ውሻውና ጥላው

Review revision: `BATCH2-AESOP-R1-DRAFT-2026-07-08-DOG-REFLECTION`
Review status: draft text only; not owner-reviewed; not text locked

English review title: **The Dog and His Reflection**
Origin basis: Aesopic public-domain tradition
Source anchor: Project Gutenberg, V. S. Vernon Jones, *Aesop's Fables*, 1912
Draft audience: ages 3-6
Draft length: 8 pages

## Page 1

**Amharic**

አንድ ደስተኛ ውሻ ትንሽ ጣፋጭ ቁራሽ አገኘ። ቁራሹን በአፉ ይዞ ወደ ቤቱ መጓዝ ጀመረ።

**English**

A happy dog found a small tasty piece of food. He carried it in his mouth and started home.

**Illustration beat:** Friendly dog carrying a small treat on a path.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

መንገዱ በጸጥ ያለ ውሃ አጠገብ ያልፍ ነበር። ውሃው እንደ መስታወት ያንጸባርቅ ነበር።

**English**

The path passed beside quiet water. The water shone like a mirror.

**Illustration beat:** Calm reflective stream beside the path.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

ውሻው ወደ ውሃው ተመለከተ። በውሃው ውስጥ ሌላ ውሻ ቁራሽ ይዞ እንደቆመ መሰለው።

**English**

Dog looked into the water. It seemed as if another dog was standing there with a piece of food.

**Illustration beat:** Dog seeing his reflection in the water.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

እርግጥ ግን ያየው የራሱ ጥላ ነበር። ውሻው ግን “የዚያ ውሻ ቁራሽ ይበልጣል ይመስላል” ብሎ አሰበ።

**English**

Really, it was only his own reflection. But Dog thought, “That dog’s piece looks bigger than mine.”

**Illustration beat:** Reflection and real dog clearly mirrored.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

“ሁለቱንም ላገኝ እችላለሁ!” ብሎ አፉን ከፈተ። በዚያን ጊዜ የያዘው ቁራሽ ከአፉ ወደ ውሃው ወደቀ።

**English**

“Maybe I can have both!” he thought, and opened his mouth. His own food fell into the water.

**Illustration beat:** Treat falling gently into stream.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ውሻው በድንጋጤ ውሃውን ተመለከተ። ጥላው ተንቀጠቀጠ፣ ቁራሹም በውሃው ላይ ርቆ ሄደ።

**English**

Dog looked at the water in surprise. The reflection wiggled, and his food floated away.

**Illustration beat:** Dog surprised, treat floating away without distress.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

ውሻው ትንሽ ቆሞ አሰበ። “የሌላ ውሻ ነው ብዬ ያየሁት የራሴ ጥላ ነበር። መጀመሪያ ብቆም ኖሮ ይሻል ነበር” አለ።

**English**

Dog stopped and thought. “What I saw was my own reflection. I should have paused first.”

**Illustration beat:** Dog sitting quietly beside water.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ከዚያ ቀን በኋላ ውሻው ያለውን ማመስገን ተማረ። አዲስ ነገር ሲመኝም መጀመሪያ ቆሞ ያስብ ነበር።

**English**

After that day, Dog learned to be thankful for what he had. When he wanted something new, he paused and thought first.

**Illustration beat:** Dog walking home calmly in warm light.

## Review questions

- Is “reflection” clear enough for ages 3-4?
- Should `ጥላ` be retained, or is another Amharic phrase better for water reflection?
- Is the lost-food consequence gentle enough, or still too sad?
