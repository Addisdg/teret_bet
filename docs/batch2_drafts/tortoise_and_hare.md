# ኤሊውና ጥንቸሉ

Review revision: `BATCH2-AESOP-R1-DRAFT-2026-07-08-TORTOISE-HARE`

English review title: **The Tortoise and the Hare**
Origin basis: Aesopic public-domain tradition
Source anchor: Project Gutenberg, V. S. Vernon Jones, *Aesop's Fables*, 1912
Draft audience: ages 3-6
Draft length: 9 pages

## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ አረንጓዴ መንገድ ላይ ኤሊው በቀስታ ይጓዝ ነበር። እርምጃው ቀስ ቢልም ሁልጊዜ ይቀጥል ነበር።

**English**

On a green path, Tortoise walked slowly. His steps were small, but he always kept going.

**Illustration beat:** Calm tortoise moving steadily on a green path.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

ጥንቸሉ በፍጥነት እየዘለለ መጣ። “ኤሊ ሆይ፣ አንተ በጣም ቀስ ትሄዳለህ!” ብሎ ሳቀ።

**English**

Hare came hopping fast. “Tortoise, you move so slowly!” he laughed.

**Illustration beat:** Hare hopping beside tortoise; playful, not cruel.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

ኤሊው በቀስታ ፈገግ አለ። “እኔ ቀስ እሄዳለሁ፣ ግን አልተውም። እስከዚያ ትልቅ ዛፍ ድረስ እንወዳደር?” አለ።

**English**

Tortoise smiled gently. “I go slowly, but I do not stop. Shall we race to that big tree?”

**Illustration beat:** Tortoise pointing toward a distant tree.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

የመንገዱ ጓደኞች “ጀምሩ!” አሉ። ጥንቸሉ ፈጥኖ ሮጠ፣ ኤሊው ግን “በቀስታ በቀስታ” እያለ ጀመረ።

**English**

Their friends called, “Start!” Hare ran fast, while Tortoise began, “Slowly, slowly.”

**Illustration beat:** Animal friends cheer; race begins.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

ጥንቸሉ በጣም ፈጥኖ ከፊት ሄደ። ወደኋላ ተመልክቶ ኤሊውን አላየም። “ትንሽ እረፍታለሁ” አለ።

**English**

Hare ran far ahead. He looked back and could not see Tortoise. “I will rest for a little while,” he said.

**Illustration beat:** Hare far ahead near flowers.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ጥንቸሉ በአበቦች አጠገብ ተቀመጠ። ዓይኑ ተከበደ፣ እንቅልፍም ወሰደው።

**English**

Hare sat beside the flowers. His eyes grew heavy, and soon he fell asleep.

**Illustration beat:** Hare sleeping peacefully beside flowers.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

ኤሊው ግን አልቆመም። “በቀስታ በቀስታ፣ እቀጥላለሁ” እያለ መንገዱን ቀጠለ።

**English**

But Tortoise did not stop. “Slowly, slowly, I will keep going,” he said.

**Illustration beat:** Tortoise passing flowers with steady expression.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ጥንቸሉ ሲነቃ ኤሊው ወደ ዛፉ ተቃርቦ ነበር። ጥንቸሉ ፈጥኖ ሮጠ፣ ኤሊው ግን መጀመሪያ ደረሰ።

**English**

When Hare woke up, Tortoise was almost at the tree. Hare ran fast, but Tortoise arrived first.

**Illustration beat:** Tortoise reaching tree while hare runs in kindly surprise.

## Page 9

**Amharic**

ጥንቸሉ “ፍጥነት ብቻ አይበቃም፤ መቀጠልም ያስፈልጋል” አለ። ኤሊውም ፈገግ አለ። ሁለቱም እንደጓደኞች ወደ ቤታቸው ተመለሱ።

**English**

“Speed alone is not enough; keeping on matters too,” said Hare. Tortoise smiled, and the two friends walked home together.

**Illustration beat:** Tortoise and hare smiling together after the race.
