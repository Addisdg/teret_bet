# ቁራውና የውሃ ማሰሮው

Review revision: `BATCH2-AESOP-R1-DRAFT-2026-07-08-CROW-PITCHER`
Review status: draft text only; not owner-reviewed; not text locked

English review title: **The Crow and the Pitcher**
Origin basis: Aesopic public-domain tradition
Source anchor: Project Gutenberg, V. S. Vernon Jones, *Aesop's Fables*, 1912
Draft audience: ages 3-6
Draft length: 8 pages

## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ ሞቃት ቀን አንድ ቁራ ውሃ ፈልጎ በሜዳ ላይ ይበር ነበር። አፉ ደርቆት ነበር።

**English**

On a warm day, Crow flew over a field looking for water. His beak felt dry.

**Illustration beat:** Crow flying over a warm dry field.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

በዛፍ አጠገብ አንድ የሸክላ ማሰሮ አየ። ቁራው በደስታ ወደ ማሰሮው ቀረበ።

**English**

Near a tree, he saw a clay pitcher. Crow flew down hopefully.

**Illustration beat:** Crow noticing a clay pitcher near a tree.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

በማሰሮው ውስጥ ትንሽ ውሃ ነበር። ግን ውሃው በጣም ዝቅ ብሎ ነበር፤ ምንቃሩም አልደረሰም።

**English**

There was a little water inside, but it was very low. Crow’s beak could not reach it.

**Illustration beat:** Low water visible inside pitcher.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

ቁራው ማሰሮውን በቀስታ ተመለከተ። “አልቸኩልም። መፍትሔ እፈልጋለሁ” አለ።

**English**

Crow looked carefully at the pitcher. “I will not rush. I will look for a solution,” he said.

**Illustration beat:** Crow thinking beside pitcher.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

ከዚያ በመሬት ላይ ትንሽ ድንጋይ አየ። ድንጋዩን አንስቶ ወደ ማሰሮው ጣለው። ፕሉፕ!

**English**

Then he saw a small pebble on the ground. He picked it up and dropped it into the pitcher. Plop!

**Illustration beat:** Crow dropping one pebble into the pitcher.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ውሃው ትንሽ ከፍ አለ። ቁራው ሌላ ድንጋይ አመጣ። አንድ ድንጋይ፣ ሌላ ድንጋይ፣ ውሃው በቀስታ ከፍ እያለ መጣ።

**English**

The water rose a little. Crow brought another pebble, then another. Little by little, the water came up.

**Illustration beat:** Sequence of pebbles raising the water.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

ብዙ ሳይቆይ ውሃው ምንቃሩ የሚደርስበት ቦታ ደረሰ። ቁራው በደስታ ጠጣ።

**English**

Soon the water rose high enough for his beak. Crow drank happily.

**Illustration beat:** Crow drinking safely from the raised water.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ቁራው “ትንሽ ትንሽ ሙከራ ትልቅ ችግኝ ይፈታል” ብሎ ተማረ። ከዚያም በተረጋጋ ልብ ወደ ሰማይ በረረ።

**English**

Crow learned that small patient steps can solve a big problem. Then he flew back into the sky with a peaceful heart.

**Illustration beat:** Crow flying away over the field.

## Review questions

- Do children understand why the pebbles make the water rise?
- Is `ፕሉፕ!` acceptable/fun in Amharic read-aloud, or should it be removed?
- Does “dry beak” read naturally, or should it be simply “በጣም ተጠምቶ ነበር”?
