## Page 1

**Amharic**

በአንድ ፀሐያማ ቀን ቀበሮው በእርሻ መንገድ ላይ ይጓዝ ነበር። ሆዱም ትንሽ ተርቦ ነበር።

**English**

One sunny day, Fox walked along a farm path. His tummy felt a little hungry.

**Illustration beat:** Fox walking on a warm path near grape vines.

## Page 2

**Amharic**

በወይን ተክል ላይ የተንጠለጠለ ጣፋጭ ወይን አየ። “እንዴት ያማረ ወይን ነው!” ብሎ አይኑን አበራ።

**English**

He saw beautiful grapes hanging from a vine. “What lovely grapes!” he said, his eyes bright.

**Illustration beat:** Fox looking up at purple grapes on a high vine.

## Page 3

**Amharic**

ቀበሮው በቀስታ ዘለለ። እግሮቹ አፈርን ነኩ፣ ወይኑ ግን ከፍ ብሎ ቀረ።

**English**

Fox jumped gently. His feet touched the ground again, but the grapes stayed high above him.

**Illustration beat:** Safe, playful jump toward unreachable grapes.

## Page 4

**Amharic**

“እንደገና እሞክራለሁ” አለ። እንደገና ዘለለ፣ እንደገናም ዘለለ። ወይኑ ግን አሁንም ከእጁ ርቆ ነበር።

**English**

“I will try again,” he said. He jumped again and again, but the grapes were still out of reach.

**Illustration beat:** Fox trying a few times without danger or frustration becoming scary.

## Page 5

**Amharic**

ቀበሮው ትንሽ ተበሳጨ። አፉን አጠፈና “እንደዚህ ከሆነ ወይኑ ጣፋጭ አይሆንም ይሆናል” አለ።

**English**

Fox felt disappointed. He made a small face and said, “Maybe those grapes are not sweet after all.”

**Illustration beat:** Fox looking disappointed, not angry.

## Page 6

**Amharic**

ከዚያ ትንሽ ቆም ብሎ አሰበ። “እውነቱ ግን ወይኑን ፈልጌ ነበር። ስላልደረስኩበት ብቻ ተከፋሁ” አለ።

**English**

Then he stopped and thought. “The truth is, I did want the grapes. I felt sad because I could not reach them.”

**Illustration beat:** Fox pausing thoughtfully beneath the vine.

## Page 7

**Amharic**

ቀበሮው በጥልቅ ተነፈሰ። “ያልተሳካልኝን ነገር መናቅ አያስፈልገኝም። ስሜቴን በእውነት መናገር እችላለሁ” አለ።

**English**

Fox took a deep breath. “I do not have to call something bad just because I missed it. I can tell the truth about my feelings.”

**Illustration beat:** Gentle emotional-learning beat, fox calm.

## Page 8

**Amharic**

ከዚያም ቀበሮው በሰላም መንገዱን ቀጠለ። “ዛሬ ወይን አላገኘሁም፤ እውነተኛ መሆንን ግን ተማርኩ” ብሎ ፈገግ አለ።

**English**

Then Fox walked on peacefully. “I did not get grapes today, but I learned to be honest,” he said with a small smile.

**Illustration beat:** Fox walking away peacefully through warm light.
