## About Teret Bet

Teret Bet is an Amharic- and English-language children's storybook app designed
for families with children ages 3-6. A parent or caregiver is expected to
operate the app and read with the child. The app does not require an account.

## Information stored on the device

Teret Bet stores the following reading preferences only on the user's device:

- selected language and display settings;
- favourite stories;
- reading position and completion state; and
- up to 20 recent reading-history entries, identified by story ID and time.

This information is used only to provide the app's reading, favourites,
Continue Reading, and settings features. Teret Bet disables Android cloud backup
and device transfer for this app data. Families can clear reading history in the
app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data under normal Android behavior.

## Network access and Firebase

Teret Bet can contact Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to retrieve the public
story catalogue and story pages. The app also includes bundled stories and
illustrations so reading remains available when the network is unavailable.

Teret Bet does not send favourites, reading history, reading position, selected
language, or display settings to Firestore. It does not create child profiles or
upload a child's writing, photos, voice, or other content.

Google may process technical connection and service information when providing
Firebase. Firebase's privacy and security information is available at
<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>, and its data-processing terms are
available at <https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms/>.

Before publication, the developer will verify the exact signed app bundle and
Firebase SDK disclosures and update this section if those services process any
additional data that must be disclosed.

## Information Teret Bet does not request

Version 1 does not request or provide features that collect:

- names, email addresses, accounts, or child profiles;
- dates of birth or precise ages;
- precise location, contacts, phone numbers, photos, camera, or microphone data;
- advertising identifiers for advertising;
- analytics or crash-reporting events;
- payment information; or
- messages, social posts, or user-generated content.

Teret Bet contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

## Children's privacy

Teret Bet is designed for young children to use with a parent or caregiver. The
app does not ask children to provide personal information. If a parent contacts
support, they should not include a child's name, photo, voice recording, exact
location, school, or other identifying information.

If the developer learns that personal information from a child was provided
through support correspondence, it will be deleted as soon as reasonably
practicable after the request or issue is handled, unless retention is required
by law.

## Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell personal information. Teret Bet does not share
personal information for advertising. Service providers may process limited
information only as needed to provide Firebase catalogue access, app
distribution, website hosting, or adult support correspondence, subject to
their terms and applicable law.

## Security

The developer uses reasonable measures appropriate to this small, account-free
app, including limiting requested permissions, keeping reading state local,
disabling Android backup for that state, and using signed application releases.
No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely
secure.
