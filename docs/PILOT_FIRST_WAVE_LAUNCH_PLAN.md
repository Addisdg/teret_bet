## Purpose

The first wave tests the highest-risk cultural, safety, language, story-flow,
and illustration questions before the pilot is circulated more widely. Send
one story per session. Keep names, contact details, family relationships, and
the code-to-person mapping in the owner's private roster outside Git.

## Launch sequence

1. Privately match `C01`, `C02`, and `C03` first.
2. Send one specialist story package and request page-specific comments.
3. Pause the affected story if attribution, cultural representation, or child
   safety is challenged.
4. If no stop finding emerges, privately match and send `R01` through `R04`.
5. Keep Amharic and English in separate sittings. This first wave is Amharic;
   schedule English sessions after its material findings are triaged.
6. De-identify returned notes before adding findings to
   `PILOT_REVIEW_FINDINGS_REGISTER.md`; summarize story-level coverage in
   `PILOT_FAMILY_REVIEW_RESULTS.md`.
7. Decide whether each story proceeds unchanged, needs R2, or triggers
   replacement review before recruiting the wider second wave.
