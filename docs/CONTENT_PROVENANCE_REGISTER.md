# Content Provenance and Rights Register

## Rule

Every story needs at least one credible documented source, preferably
corroborated by a second. Traditional story motifs may be retold in original
words; a modern collector's wording, translation, exercises, and illustrations
remain protected unless their licence or written permission allows reuse.

For Ethiopian material, record the storyteller, community/region, collector,
collection context, language, URL or publication, and human cultural reviewer.
Do not generalise a community-specific story as representing all Ethiopia.

## Candidate source

The Ethiopian Story Collecting Project was organised with Ethiopia's Ministry
of Education, the British Council, regional cultural and educational bureaus,
and Elizabeth Laird. Its traditional tales may be retold in original wording,
but its published versions and illustrations must not be reproduced without
permission. Verify current terms at:

- https://ethiopianfolktales.com/
- https://ethiopianfolktales.com/en/the-project/copyright-and-translation
