# Pilot review illustration assets

These assets are visual-review candidates for the five-story family pilot.
They are not release-final artwork. Keep them separate from the bundled story
catalogue until text, family, cultural, visual, and owner review are complete.

Source and provenance limitations are recorded in
`docs/PILOT_ILLUSTRATION_PROVENANCE_R1_2026-07-05.md`. These assets may be used
for review, but none may enter the release catalogue while its provenance or
rights status remains unresolved.

Exact revised prompts for the project-generated PNGs are mapped by asset path in
`docs/pilot_illustration_prompts_r1.json`. Only the current `_r1.png` review
assets belong in these story folders. Superseded legacy WebP review copies were
removed after corrected PNG coverage and packet checksums were verified; their
catalogue originals and external original-image backup remain preserved.
